# Phase 2 - Email, SMS, Calendar, and CRM Integrations

Prepare integrations for operational CRM use.

Areas:
- Email sending provider
- SMS sending provider
- Calendar scheduling provider
- Contact import/export
- CRM data import
- Unsubscribe/opt-out handling
- Message logs
- Delivery/error events

Safety:
- Bulk sends require approval/permission where appropriate.
- SMS opt-outs must be respected.
- Email unsubscribe must be respected.
- No fake sends.
- No fake delivery metrics.
